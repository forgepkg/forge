**Breaking:** every account is named by a UPN, `local-part@domain`
(`alice@example.com`). `POST /admin/users` answers 400 for a name that is no
UPN ("username must be a UPN such as alice@example.com"), and an `oidc` realm
provisions an account only when its claim chain (username claim, verified
email, `sub`) yields a UPN; otherwise the login is refused and the login page
names the claim. Entra's `preferred_username` is the UPN; with another IdP,
map a claim that holds one. New OIDC accounts are no longer namespaced while
a credential-forwarding realm is enabled.
