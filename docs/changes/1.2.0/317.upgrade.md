**Breaking:** every new account is named by a UPN, `local-part@domain`
(`alice@example.com`). `POST /admin/users` answers 400 for any other name
("username must be a UPN such as alice@example.com"), and an `oidc` realm
provisions an account only when its claim chain (username claim, verified
email, `sub`) yields a UPN; otherwise the login is refused and the login page
names the claim. Entra's `preferred_username` is the UPN; with another IdP,
map a claim that holds one. Credential-forwarding accounts keep the name
their upstream accepted. Existing accounts (`alice`, `admin`) keep their
names, passwords, tokens and roles, and **Admin → Users** marks them
"legacy name". While a credential-forwarding realm is enabled, a new OIDC
account is named `bob+<realm>@corp.example` instead of
`bob@corp.example@<realm>`, and a taken name gets its `-2` on the local part.
