The Helm chart (`metrics.enabled`) and the kustomize base
(`FORGE_SERVER__METRICS_BIND`) now open the metrics port 9421 on the Forge
pods and their Service. The NetworkPolicy of the chart and of
`components/network-policy` admit scrapers to it (the chart's from
`networkPolicy.metricsFrom`, everyone when empty; the component's from the
placeholder namespace `monitoring`, to patch). Set `metrics.enabled: false`,
or drop the variable, to keep the port closed.
