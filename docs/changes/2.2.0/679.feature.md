Docker proxies serve a layer they do not hold yet while they download it,
instead of after it is downloaded, verified and stored: `docker pull`
receives the layer as it arrives from the upstream, and other pulls of it
on the same instance meanwhile are served from that one download. A layer
is still cached only once it matches its digest, and its last byte is sent
only then: a layer that does not match ends the response short, an error
the client retries. The download finishes and is cached also when its
client goes away. Image configs (at most 1 MiB) and layers whose upstream
sends no `Content-Length` are downloaded before they are served, as before.
