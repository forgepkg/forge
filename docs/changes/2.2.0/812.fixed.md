An administrator made under **Admin → Users → New user** or through
`POST /admin/users` with `"role": "admin"`, or switched to Admin on the
user's page, now holds the built-in `reader` role as well, as the
administrators the upgrade finds do. One whose identity provider or upstream
later takes `admin` away stays a reader, instead of being left with no
built-in role (Roles only) and losing all read access. Service accounts are
never administrators and are not given `reader`.
