With the identifier-first login screen, an account with a password whose
username is in an OIDC realm's login domain could not sign in from the
browser: the screen sent it to the identity provider and never asked for
the password. That locked out an administrator who added a realm with their
own domain. While a password realm is enabled, the screen now offers **Sign
in with a password instead**, for every username alike, and so does the
notice of a sign-in the identity provider refused or failed. The routing by
login domain and the combined screen are unchanged.
