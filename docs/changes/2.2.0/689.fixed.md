A Docker proxy whose upstream lost the connection, or stalled past its
timeouts, in the middle of a blob of at most 1 MiB (an image config) or of
one sent without a `Content-Length` answered 500 and logged a storage
error; it answers 503, `the upstream registry is unreachable`, and logs a
warning. An image import's item that fails the same way names the source
registry as unreachable instead of reading `internal error`.
