A Docker group's pages labelled the times of manifests served by a proxy
member "Pushed", though they are when Forge cached them from the upstream.
An image's manifests now say "Cached" or "Pushed" by the member each comes
from, and "Pushed / cached" with a label on each row where the members mix;
a group's image list, which names no member, heads its column "Last pushed /
cached".
