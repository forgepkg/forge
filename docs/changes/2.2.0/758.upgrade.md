From the upgrade on, every instance counts the requests it serves for
**Admin → Traffic** and records the client address of each, without an
administrator turning anything on. The addresses are personal data (under
the GDPR, for one): an IPv6 client is kept by its /64 alone, and the
statistics, addresses included, are kept 14 days by default. Turn
**Client addresses** or **Count requests** off, or set another retention,
under **Admin → Traffic → Settings** (`/admin/settings/traffic`); with
addresses off, requests are still counted by repository, country and
autonomous system.
