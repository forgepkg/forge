apt repositories for Debian and Ubuntu packages (`"format": "apt"`, served at
`/repository/<name>/` like Nexus'): hosted repositories take `.deb` uploads
(Nexus' `curl --data-binary` `POST`, a form or a `PUT`) and publish them as
one distribution, the repository's `distribution` option, with `InRelease`,
`Release.gpg` and `Packages` indexes (also `.gz`, `.xz` and `by-hash`) signed
by Ed25519 keys of the repository's own, which **Admin → Repositories**
generates, imports and rotates; proxies cache a Debian or Ubuntu mirror's
distribution, passing its signed indexes on unchanged and checking packages
against them. Each repository's page shows its `sources.list` line, its key
and its `auth.conf.d` entry, and browses its distribution by component and
architecture ([apt repositories](docs/apt.md)).
