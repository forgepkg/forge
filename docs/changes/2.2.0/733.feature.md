The access level Reader is a role now: the built-in `reader` role reads
every repository, ones created later included, as `writer` publishes to
every one. The level without a built-in role, where only the account's
other roles count, is **Roles only**. An OIDC or trusted-header realm that
creates accounts gives them the level its **Access level of new accounts**
names (`new_account_access`: `reader`, the default, or `roles_only`), on its
page under **Admin → Authentication**.
