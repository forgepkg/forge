A NuGet package of more than 10 000 entries was refused (`limit: … entries
exceeds max 10000`), whether it was published, proxied or imported from
Nexus, and the limit could not go much higher: to read the package's
manifest, Forge kept an index of every entry in memory, about half a GiB for
a million. It now reads the zip directory one entry at a time and inflates
the manifest alone, in the same memory whatever the entry count, and the
`[nuget]` defaults are far higher: `max_entries` 1 000 000 (was 10 000) and
`max_central_directory_bytes` 256 MiB (was 16 MiB). The items of a Nexus
import that failed on the limit are copied by **Retry failed items** on the
import's page.
