A NuGet package whose zip says it is one part of a split or spanned archive
was stored and listed, though no NuGet client installs it ("Split or spanned
archives are not supported"): an end record that names another disk for the
start of its central directory or whose two entry counts differ, a ZIP64
end record whose two entry counts differ, or a manifest whose entry starts
on another disk than the archive's. A push or an import of such a package
is now refused, and a proxy no longer stores or serves it from its
upstream. 2.1 refused only the first of these, and a ZIP64 record with
more entries on its disk than in all.
