Retention's `prerelease_superseded_for_days` counted only from the
superseding release's publication, so a prerelease published after its
release (a lagging CI version bump, a maintenance line's `1.9.1-rc.1` after
`2.0.0`) was deleted on the next run. Its clock now starts at the later of
the two publications: a prerelease always has its N days.
