An apt import kept every entry of the source's `Packages` index in memory,
about 16 times the inflated index, so a hostile or compromised source could
make it take tens of GiB and get the instance killed. The entries of an
index may take at most 256 MiB, far above what a real index takes (Debian
main for amd64, about 30 MiB); the import of a larger one fails, saying so.
