The login screen remembers the accounts signed in on a device and offers them
the next time, like Microsoft's "Pick an account": one click continues at the
password step, or at the identity provider, which now gets the account as
`login_hint` and does not ask for it again (the email typed into the
identifier-first form is passed the same way). **Use another account** shows
the usual form, and an account can be forgotten. The browser keeps at most
five, never a password or a session. Kiosks and shared computers turn it off
with **Remember accounts on the login screen** under **Admin → Settings**
(`remember_accounts` in `/admin/settings/auth`).
