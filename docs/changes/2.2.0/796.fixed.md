A group member scoped to package patterns made every group search ask that
proxy's upstream for 1,000 hits, where an unscoped proxy is asked for about
32. A scoped member is asked for the same window of its upstream's hits as an
unscoped one, and its count is estimated from the share of them its scope
admits; a search whose `packageid:` names no id in the member's scope does
not ask its upstream at all. A member with a content selector is still asked
for every hit.
