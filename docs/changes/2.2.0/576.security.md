Docker: the config and layers of a deleted image, manifest or tag stayed
pullable by digest for as long as someone fetched them once within
`docker.unreferenced_blob_ttl_seconds` (24 h), since every `GET` or `HEAD`
of a blob no manifest references renewed its grace period: anyone who could
read the repository and knew a digest kept the content of a deleted image
alive. A fetch renews it only for a client that may push the image (a `HEAD`
with a push token, as `docker push` sends before it uploads a layer), so the
registry sweeper releases such a blob at most that TTL after it was last
pushed or mounted, however often it is pulled. The image page's delete
dialog and [docs/docker.md](docs/docker.md#retention-and-garbage-collection)
say what stays pullable and for how long.
