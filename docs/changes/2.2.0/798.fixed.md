An apt proxy of an upstream without `Acquire-By-Hash` (reprepro's and
aptly's default, packagecloud, many vendor repositories) could serve an
`InRelease` and a `Packages` from two different publishes after the
upstream published, and `apt-get update` failed with "Hash Sum mismatch"
until the metadata TTL passed. Each index was re-validated on its own
clock. An index that the `InRelease` served lists with another size or
SHA-256 is now re-validated before it is served, and once `InRelease` or
`Release` is replaced, the distribution's other indexes (`Release.gpg`
too) are re-validated on their next download. Debian and Ubuntu archives,
which apt reads by hash, are served as before.
