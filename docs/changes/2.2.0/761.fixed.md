A writer could break a hosted apt repository's indexes for every reader:
packages of many components and made-up architectures took the index past
the 10,000 files it may hold, and from then on `InRelease`, `Packages` and
`key.asc` answered 500. An `Architecture` is now `all` or one dpkg knows
(`dpkg-architecture -L`), and a repository holds at most 8 components: an
upload of another architecture, or of a ninth component, answers 400
naming the limit, and an import reports such a package as failed.
