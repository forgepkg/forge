A credential-forwarding realm that accepts no passwords from clients
(`accept_client_passwords: false`) let its users' passwords go to the next
credential-forwarding realm's upstream: when one of its users signed a client
in with their password (HTTP Basic on the feeds and `/v2/token`,
`npm login --auth-type=legacy`), a credential-forwarding realm after it in the
realm order forwarded the name and the password to its own upstream. The
sign-in was refused, but the password had reached a server that is not its
realm's. Such a realm now keeps its accounts' names in the clients' chain:
their passwords are refused there like a wrong one, without counting towards
the sign-in lockout, and never reach another realm's upstream, as a local
realm's passwords never do.
