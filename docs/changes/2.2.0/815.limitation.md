An apt upload or import refuses a package whose control member dpkg-deb
compressed with zstd level 20 or higher (`-Zzstd -z20`) and that is larger
than about 128 KiB: it declares a window of 32 MiB or more, which the
decoder would hold whole for each such upload. Levels up to 19 and xz at
any level are read ([apt repositories](docs/apt.md#limitations)).
