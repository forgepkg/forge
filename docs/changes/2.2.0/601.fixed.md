One upstream operation of a proxy (a version listing, dating the package
delay) keeps at most 4 × `proxy.max_metadata_bytes` of documents, but a
document counted only once it had been downloaded, cached and decoded. With
an upstream serving documents near the limit, the four fetches that run at
once each held theirs and the copies made to cache it, beside what the
operation kept already: about 19 × `proxy.max_metadata_bytes` (600 MiB by
default) where the documentation said 4 ×. A document's bytes are now taken
from the operation's budget before it is read into memory, and its download
stops at what is left: one that does not fit is not read. An operation holds
at most 4 × `proxy.max_metadata_bytes` of documents, the ones it is reading
included, and 4/3 × more while it caches one: 5 1/3 × (171 MiB by default),
which `docs/deployment.md` (Memory) now says.
