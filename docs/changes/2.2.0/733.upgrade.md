The upgrade gives the built-in `reader` role to every user who held neither
`admin` nor `writer`: they read every repository from then on. Service
accounts keep only their own roles (Roles only). A role an administrator
had named `reader` is renamed `reader-<id>`, its grants and members kept.
`POST /admin/users` without a `role` now makes a user a reader and a
service account roles only; `"role": "roles_only"` gives no built-in role.
A realm that creates accounts makes them readers unless its **Access level
of new accounts** is set to Roles only.
